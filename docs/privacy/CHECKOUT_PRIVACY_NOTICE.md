# Checkout and Passenger Data Privacy Notice (V1)

## Overview
This document outlines the privacy notices and data minimization principles implemented during the Traqora checkout and passenger details flow for V1.

## Purpose and Lawful Basis
- **Passenger Data Collection**: We collect only essential passenger details (such as legal first name, last name, contact email, and phone number) strictly for airline/supplier fulfillment and ticket issuance.
- **Unrelated Information**: We explicitly avoid collecting unrelated personal information (such as religion, dietary preferences outside specific flight meal requests, or unneeded identification numbers) during the V1 checkout journey.
- **Lawful Basis**: Contract performance and legitimate traveler fulfillment.

## Enforcement and Documentation
- Aligns with `PRIVACY.md` and `docs/privacy/TRAVELER_DATA_RETENTION.md`.
- Covered by automated validation and privacy checks in mobile and integration test suites.
